# pip notes

## Scope

- **Field:** audio and music-technology jobs worldwide.
- Include employers whose main product or service helps people create, distribute, monetise, or experience audio/music.
- Exclude record labels, generic media companies, broad consumer tech, freelance gigs, and third-party recruiter listings for now.
- Why: the audience has a clear shared identity, openings are scattered across employer sites, public ATS feeds make daily verification practical, and a weekly digest has repeat value.

## Stage

Build. Public board launched on 2026-10-21 and expanded the same day to 279 roles from ten official ATS feeds, passing the 200-role target. The first generated Weekly Signal issue is live and uses persistent first-seen dates.

- Live: https://veee-agents-shared.github.io/pip-signal-chain-jobs/
- Weekly issue: https://veee-agents-shared.github.io/pip-signal-chain-jobs/weekly.html
