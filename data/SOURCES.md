# Sources

All collection is from employers' public applicant-tracking-system feeds. Greenhouse documents that its GET job-board data is public and requires no authentication; Lever documents its Postings API as a way to create a job site and states published posts are publicly viewable. Checked 2026-10-21.

| Employer | Official public feed | Why in scope |
|---|---|---|
| Splice | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/splice/jobs` | Cloud platform for music creation and sounds |
| AudioMob | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/audiomob/jobs` | Audio advertising technology |
| Descript | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/descript/jobs` | Audio and video editing software |
| SoundCloud | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/soundcloud71/jobs` | Music streaming and artist platform |
| DistroKid | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/distrokid/jobs` | Digital music distribution |
| Fender | `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/fender/jobs` | Musical instruments and music-learning technology |
| Audinate | `api.lever.co/v0/postings/audinate?mode=json` | Professional networked audio technology |

API documentation: [Greenhouse Job Board API](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html), [Lever Postings API](https://github.com/lever/postings-api).
